# Walkthrough

About four minutes. Start at https://proactivos-demo-rd.fly.dev (`?lang=en` for English). Overview, data and code: [README.md](./README.md).

To start clean, press **Reiniciar demostración** at the bottom of [Mis autorizaciones](https://pdpp-demo-rd.fly.dev/owner/autorizaciones) first.

1. **The offer.** Servicios Proactivos: "Cuando nazca su bebé, podemos organizar sus vacunas y el bono por hijo… sin que tenga que solicitarlos." It says what it will need (prenatal record from SNS, household file from SIUBEN), why, and until when. Nothing is shared yet. Click **Decir sí con Cuenta Única**.
2. **Sign in.** The Cuenta Única-style sign-in names the service she is continuing to. The cédula is pre-filled; type the password.
3. **The one screen.** Who is asking (Servicios Proactivos), for what purpose, from which institutions (SNS, SIUBEN), which fields, until when ("Hasta el 31 de enero de 2027"). Only what is on this screen is shared.
   Optional: untick **Miembros del hogar registrados** and the **Centro de salud** field. Fields the service needs (such as the due date) stay locked.
   Click **Autorizar**.
4. **The grant, in use.** Back at Servicios Proactivos: the due date from SNS and the household classification from SIUBEN arrived; nobody carried a paper. Anything unticked is marked "No lo compartió" and never left the institution. The authorization card shows the purpose and the end date.
5. **See it and cancel it.** Click **Ver o revocar en Mis autorizaciones**: what she allowed, until when, and what was read and when. Click **Revocar**.
6. **Cut off.** Back on Servicios Proactivos, **Volver a consultar** is refused. The service keeps only the copy it already received.
